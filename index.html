What software does each player actually need?

Six different kinds of company are on the map — each buys (or builds) a very different software stack. This is the demand side of India's chip-software gap. The green notes mark where open-source alternatives already exist today.

🧠 Fabless chip startups

Mindgrove, Netrasemi, InCore, BigEndian, Aheesa, iVP Semi, ~100 more
  • RTL design & simulation — Synopsys VCS / Cadence Xcelium
  • Synthesis & place-and-route — Fusion Compiler / Innovus
  • Analog design — Cadence Virtuoso (₹30 Cr+ class licences)
  • Signoff — PrimeTime (STA), StarRC (parasitics), Calibre (DRC/LVS)
  • Verification — UVM frameworks, FPGA prototyping, emulation
Open-source today: Yosys + OpenLane/OpenROAD full digital flow, ngspice/Xyce analog, Verilator, cocotb, OpenSTA. PDK access via ChipIN Centre; MPW shuttles via DLI.

🏭 Wafer fabs

Tata–PSMC (Dholera, 28nm), SCL Mohali (180nm, being modernised)
  • MES / manufacturing execution — Applied SmartFactory
  • Fault detection & classification (FDC), SPC, yield management
  • Computational lithography / OPC — Synopsys & Cadence class
  • PDK development & design enablement for customers (TCAD)
  • Equipment automation — SECS/GEM interfaces, scheduling, ERP
The gap: every one of these is foreign today. Tata is co-developing EDA/TCAD with Synopsys; smart-manufacturing (Fab.da) is being sold into India's new fabs — the single largest untapped Indian-software opportunity.

📦 Assembly, test & packaging (ATMP/OSAT)

Micron Sanand, CG Semi, Kaynes, Tata TSAT, SPEL, RRP Electronics
  • ATP manufacturing execution & traceability systems
  • Test program development on ATE — Advantest SmarTest, Teradyne
  • Package design — Cadence SiP/Allegro, Siemens Xpedition
  • Wafer-level & advanced packaging design kits (Deca etc.)
  • Yield & test-data analytics, reliability lab software
Six+ new OSAT plants are under construction — every one imports its core software. Test/yield analytics is the fastest credible Indian entry point.

🏢 MNC design centres (GCCs)

Intel, AMD, Qualcomm, NVIDIA, TI, Samsung, NXP, Micron (India GCCs)
  • Full commercial EDA stacks (they are the EDA vendors' biggest customers)
  • Internal CAD/flow-automation teams — Python/TCL around tools
  • Regression farms, compute & licence-management dashboards
  • Emulation/pilot-lab operations, post-silicon validation
The single biggest concentration of "chip software" jobs in India: thousands of engineers write internal tooling, not chips. This talent pool is the natural seed for Indian EDA-tool companies.

🔧 Equipment & process-control R&D

Applied Materials & Lam Research (Bengaluru), KLA (Chennai)
  • Tool control & embedded firmware for fab equipment
  • Data pipelines, e-diagnostics, predictive maintenance
  • AI/ML for process control & inspection (KLA Chennai: ₹300 Cr software+AI hub, ~1,300 engineers)
Foreign firms are already building their equipment software in India — proof the skill exists; the products simply aren't Indian.

🤝 Design & test services

Tessolve, Sasken, eInfochips, MosChip, SmartSoC, HCLTech
  • Commercial EDA licences (often via customer or ChipIN)
  • In-house test-program & automation IP
  • Post-silicon validation lab software
Services revenue funds the transition to products — the classic path: automate your own services first, productise second.

Start here — which software to build, and how long it takes

Ranked by (credible entry for a new Indian builder × market timing). Each row shows two honest timelines: the fastest path to a useful MVP you can put in front of users, and the slowest path — what it takes to reach production/sign-off grade that companies will actually pay for.

Bars use a logarithmic scale from 3 months to 15 years. "Production-grade" for sign-off EDA means silicon-validated trust — the reason incumbents defend ~85% share.

Who's building chip software in India today

The Indian side of the gap — companies already writing software for chip design, test or manufacturing. It's a short list; that's the point.

InCore Semiconductors
Chennai · IIT-M Shakti spinoff
RISC-V processor IP plus SoC-design automation tools — generator-based chip design is a genuinely Indian EDA approach. Silicon-proven 180nm–16nm.
Vayavya Labs
Belagavi, Karnataka
Device-driver and embedded-tool generation (ESL) — one of the few Indian EDA-product companies; customers have included Synopsys.
Tattvam AI
Seed-funded 2026 ($1.7M)
AI systems to automate EDA-tool workflows — aiming to compress chip design iterations from years to weeks.
Tessolve · SmartSoC · MosChip
Bengaluru / Hyderabad
Test-program development, post-silicon validation labs and design automation — services-led software IP.
ChipIN Centre (C-DAC)
Bengaluru · MeitY
National EDA platform: 105 startups + 350+ institutions, 87 lakh tool-hours. The software backbone of India's design ecosystem — publicly funded, centralised.
KLA India · Applied Materials · Lam
Chennai / Bengaluru
Process-control and equipment software written in India — for foreign parents. The talent exists; the products don't (yet) fly an Indian flag.
Morphing Machines
Bengaluru · IISc
REDEFINE reconfigurable compute requires its own compiler & toolchain — deep software embedded in a silicon product.
Synopsys · Cadence · Siemens EDA (India)
Bengaluru · Noida · Hyderabad
The incumbents' India R&D is enormous — roughly a third of Synopsys' global workforce, ~26% of Siemens EDA's. They're not the gap; they're the proof of it.

Live prototypes built for this project

Two working browser tools were built alongside this map, as proof that the fastest paths below are real. Deploy them next to this page (same folder) and the links go live.

⚡ Timing Studio — a static timing analysis engine

Parses real Liberty (.lib) timing libraries and gate-level Verilog netlists, propagates rise/fall arrivals & slews through NLDM tables, runs setup/hold checks, reports WNS/TNS and critical paths — the PrimeTime/OpenSTA class of tool, in one HTML file.

🔬 SPICE Studio — an analog circuit simulator

Browser SPICE simulator with transient & DC analysis, diodes/MOSFETs/subcircuits, waveform plots and CSV export — verified against hand-computed circuits. The teaching-grade end of the analog-sim opportunity.
